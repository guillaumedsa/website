# Creamy Clam Chowder

**Servings:** 4  
**Est. Cook Time:** 45 mins

## Ingredients
- 2.50 pounds clams
- 1.00 cup clam juice
- 0.50 pound bacon
- 2.00 tablespoons butter
- 1.00 medium onion
- 2.00 stalks celery
- 1.00 quart whole milk
- 1.50 pounds potato
- 2.00 leaves bay leaves
- 1.00 cup heavy cream
-  to taste salt
-  to taste pepper

## Preparation Steps
1. Fry bacon
2. Add onions, celery, and butter
3. Add clam juice and bring to a boil
4. Add live clams and cook until opened
5. Simmer with potatoes, bay leaves, and milk
6. Blend broth
7. Combine solids, bacon, heavy cream, and clams
